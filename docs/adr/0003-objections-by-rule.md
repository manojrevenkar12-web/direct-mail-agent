# 3. Objections reach operations by a rule as well as by the model

A business that objected to marketing must not get another letter. In the evaluation the simulated model missed
about one objection email in twelve, and each miss meant a letter to someone who had said stop. A regular
expression now finds objection emails from customer service and opens a task for operations whether or not the model
did. This is one place where a for-loop is the right tool, so both run.
